== Era structure ==

An '''era''' is a macro-layer recurrence shape <code>M(L, c, [1])</code> with <code>L ≠ []</code> and cursor <code>c ≥ 4</code> (see <code>EraStartConfig</code> in <code>era.lean</code>; <code>R = [1]</code> at every boundary).

* '''Era 0 start''': raw step 43, <code>M([1], 4, [1])</code>, reached from the blank tape via <code>init_to_macro</code> = Init + EraToM(4) + SweepS (see <code>macro.txt</code>).
* '''Era end''': <code>EraDone</code> (<code>macro_era_complete</code>) collapses <code>[..., a+1, (0), 1]</code> into the solo-cursor config <code>[..., (a+6)]</code>.
* '''Next era start''': <code>SweepR</code> (<code>macro_sweep_right_empty</code>) fires immediately on that solo cursor, producing <code>M(L, c+1, [1])</code> with <code>c+1 ≥ 4</code>. The dispatcher fuses these two into the compound <code>era_and_sweep</code>.

=== Empirical era data (era-sim, see <code>era_findings.md</code>) ===

* '''63 765 era boundaries''' logged in <code>era_full.jsonl</code> over ~100 G macro steps; bit-identical to the Python simulator over 1 M macro steps.
* Every boundary has <code>kind = M</code>, <code>R = [1]</code>, <code>c ≥ 4</code>.
* Max observed <code>|L| = 19</code>; all <code>L</code> entries fit in u64; <code>L = [1]</code> only at era 0.
* No periodicity: distinct <code>(L, c)</code> at every recorded era.
* Within an era: <code>c</code> decreases by 2 per <code>Sweep</code> until <code>c ∈ {2, 3}</code>, then <code>SweepE</code>/<code>sweep_and_shift</code> exits to an <code>M0</code>-cursor phase that runs through <code>Bounce</code>/<code>multi_bounce</code> chains until <code>EraDone</code> fires.

=== Why the older "all-1s, D at rightmost 1" definition fails ===

The narrow tape-level shape <code>E_Config n</code> (all-1s tape with state D on the rightmost 1) only occurs at raw step 19 (<code>E_Config 4</code>), as part of the startup that produces era 0. After that, the tape never returns to an all-1s, D-at-rightmost form — yet the machine still has a clean recurrence at the macro layer (the <code>M(_, _, [1])</code> shape above). Earlier wiki claims of E_Config events at steps 84 / tape lengths 13, 27, 77 confused incidental all-1s tape moments with the era-boundary shape.
