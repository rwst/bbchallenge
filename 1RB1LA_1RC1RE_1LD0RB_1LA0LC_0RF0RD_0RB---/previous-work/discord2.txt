Autumn Pan:
\begin{gather}
            N(2n+1)=\frac{2(3^{2m_0+1}) + 2m_0 + 6}{2^{v_2(2(3^{2m_0+1}+2m_0+6))}} \\
            =\frac{2(3^{2m_0+1} + m_0 + 3)}{2^{v_2(2(3^{2m_0+1}+m_0+3))}} \\
            =\frac{2(3^{2m_0+1} + m_0 + 3)}{2^{1+v_2(3^{2m_0+1}+m_0+3)}} \\
            =\frac{2(3^{2m_0+1} + m_0 + 3)}{2^{1+v_2((3^{2m_0+1}+3)+m_0)}}
        \end{gather}
        If $m_0$ is odd, then the sum $(3^{2m_0+1}+3)+m_0$ will also be odd by lemma 1.2. The 2-adic valuation of this must then be zero for all $m_0=2m_1+1$, and so
        \begin{align}
            \forall i=4m_1+3\text{, }\frac{2(3^{4m_1+3} + 2m_1 + 4)}{2}
        \end{align}
        Assuming $m_0=2m_1$,
        \begin{gather}
            \frac{2(3^{2m_0+1} + m_0 + 3)}{2^{1+v_2((3^{2m_0+1}+3)+m_0)}} \\
            = \frac{2(3^{4m_1+1} + 2m_1 + 3)}{2^{1+v_2((3^{4m_1+1}+3)+2m_1)}} \\
            = \frac{2(3^{4m_1+1} + 2m_1 + 3)}{2^{1+v_2(2(\frac{3^{4m_1+1}+3}{2}+m_1))}} \\
            = \frac{2(3^{4m_1+1} + 2m_1 + 3)}{2^{1+1+v_2(\frac{3^{4m_1+1}+3}{2}+m_1)}} 
        \end{gather}
